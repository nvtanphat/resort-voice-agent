# Furama Resort Danang

## Key Specifications & Facts
- **Amenity Feature**: Guest rooms include an in-room minibar with tea/coffee (Minibar Availability)
- **Amenity Feature**: WiFi is available in guest rooms (Wifi Availability)
- **Policy Rule**: 14:00 (Check In Time)
- **Policy Rule**: 11:00 (Check Out Time)
- **Policy Rule**: Departures after 18:00 are charged one night and are subject to availability (Late Checkout After 18)
- **Policy Rule**: 50% daily room rate until 18:00; one night after 18:00; subject to availability (Late Checkout Policy)
- **Policy Rule**: Late check-out until 18:00 is charged at 50% of the daily room rate and is subject to availability (Late Checkout Until 18)
