# In-Room Dining (Room Service)

## Key Specifications & Facts
- **Internal Telephone Extension**: **Ext 10** (front_desk_contact)
- **Operating Hours**: 22:30 - 06:00 (Late Night Dishes)
- **Operating Hours**: 06:30 - 00:00 (Operating Hours)
- **Policy Rule**: Outside food/drinks and cooking in-room are not allowed for safety and hygiene reasons (Outside Food Policy)
- **Service Feature**: International and local cuisines are delivered to the guest room (Room Delivery)
